Chocolate Chip Cookies



Golden Chocolate Chip Cookies with crisp edges, soft and chewy middles and loaded with chocolate chips throughout. These perfectly balanced Cookies are made with brown and white sugar and melted butter creating the perfect crisp to chewy Cookie. Mix the dough, then chill it for 30 minutes if time allows. Scoop and bake until the edges are golden and the centers are still slightly soft. Bake until the edges are just set and the middle still looks underdone, and they will continue to cook on the outside as they cool and are best served still warm from the oven with semisweet Chocolate Chips melted throughout.



Photo credit: Butternut Bakery blog

.:: Ingredients ::.

  • 1 1/3 cups (170g) all purpose flour, spooned and leveled
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup (112g) unsalted butter, melted and cooled
  • 1/2 cup (100g) light brown sugar, packed
  • 1/3 cup (70g) granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 1/2 cups (250g) semisweet chocolate chips (I like Trader Joe’s, Guittard, or Ghirardelli) + more for topping
  • Flaky sea salt


.:: Equipment ::.

  • 2 oz Scoop
  • Large Baking Sheet
  • Mixing Bowls


.:: Step-by-Step ::.

  1. Whisk butter, brown sugar, and sugar.
  2. Whisk in egg, yolk, and vanilla.
  3. Stir in flour, baking soda, and salt.
  4. Fold in chocolate chips.
  5. Chill dough 30 minutes if you have time.
  6. Heat oven to 350°F. Line baking sheets with parchment.
  7. Scoop 2-tablespoon balls, 2 inches apart.
  8. Bake 10 to 12 minutes, until edges are golden and centers look slightly underbaked.
  9. Cool on the pan 5 minutes.
